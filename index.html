About Safari & City UG Ltd

Safari & City UG Ltd is a Ugandan-owned tour and travel company based in Kampala. We create authentic safari, city, culture and adventure experiences across Uganda — from gorilla trekking in Bwindi, to hiking the Rwenzoris, to rafting the Nile and exploring the vibrant streets of Kampala.

Our team of professional guides, drivers and travel experts ensures every trip is safe, well-planned, affordable and unforgettable — for solo travellers, families, groups and corporate clients.
